🕰️ Journey Through Deep Time

The age of dinosaurs is split into three periods together called the Mesozoic Era.

Triassic

252 – 201 million years ago

Life recovers from the worst mass extinction ever. The first dinosaurs appear around 230 million years ago, and most of them are small, speedy two-legged hunters. All land is joined in one supercontinent, Pangaea.

Jurassic

201 – 145 million years ago

Pangaea starts splitting up. Giant long-necked sauropods like Brachiosaurus and Diplodocus spread everywhere, Stegosaurus and Allosaurus appear, and the first bird, Archaeopteryx, takes off.

Cretaceous

145 – 66 million years ago

The heyday of dinosaurs: T. rex, Triceratops, Velociraptor, Spinosaurus, and armored ankylosaurs. Flowering plants appear and bees arrive along with them.

💥 The Impact

66 million years ago

An asteroid roughly 10 km wide slams into what is now Chicxulub, Mexico. About three quarters of all species are wiped out, including every non-bird dinosaur.

🐦 Today

Right now

Dinosaurs never fully left. Birds are living theropod dinosaurs, which makes the chicken in your sandwich a distant cousin of T. rex.

🙅 Myth vs. Fact

Movies got a lot of things wrong. Here's what scientists actually think.

Myth

Velociraptors were human-sized.

Fact

Real Velociraptor was about the size of a turkey and covered in feathers. The movie "raptors" are closer to Deinonychus.

Myth

Pterodactyls and plesiosaurs were dinosaurs.

Fact

Flying pterosaurs and swimming plesiosaurs and mosasaurs were reptile cousins of dinosaurs, not dinosaurs themselves.

Myth

Dinosaurs and humans lived together.

Fact

The last non-bird dinosaurs died about 66 million years before the first humans showed up.

Myth

T. rex could only see you if you moved.

Fact

T. rex had forward-facing eyes and probably better depth perception than a hawk. Standing still wouldn't save you.

Myth

Dinosaurs were all slow, scaly, and dumb.

Fact

Many were active, warm-blooded, feathered, and clever. Troodon had one of the biggest brains for its body size.

Myth

Dinosaurs are completely extinct.

Fact

Birds are dinosaurs. There are more living dinosaur species today than there are species of mammals.
