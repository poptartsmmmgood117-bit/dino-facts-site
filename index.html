03 · Deep Time

The Mesozoic

Three periods. One supercontinent breaking apart. One very bad afternoon at the end.

252 – 201 million years ago

Triassic

Life claws back from the worst mass extinction in Earth's history. The first dinosaurs appear around 230 million years ago: small, fast, two-legged hunters on a single supercontinent, Pangaea.

201 – 145 million years ago

Jurassic

Pangaea tears apart. Sauropods like Brachiosaurus and Diplodocus grow to staggering sizes, Allosaurus rules as top predator, and Archaeopteryx takes to the air.

145 – 66 million years ago

Cretaceous

The golden age: T. rex, Triceratops, Spinosaurus, Velociraptor and the armored ankylosaurs. Flowering plants spread across the planet.

66 million years ago

Impact

A rock about 10 km wide hits what is now Chicxulub, Mexico. Around three quarters of all species die, including every dinosaur that couldn't fly.

Today

Survivors

Birds are living theropod dinosaurs. The line never ended. It just got smaller and learned to sing.

04 · Corrections

Myth vs. Fact

Hollywood got a lot wrong. Here's what the fossils say.

Myth

Velociraptors were human-sized.

Fact

The real Velociraptor was turkey-sized and feathered. The movie "raptors" are closer to Deinonychus.

Myth

Pterodactyls and plesiosaurs were dinosaurs.

Fact

Pterosaurs, plesiosaurs and mosasaurs were reptile cousins of the dinosaurs, not dinosaurs themselves.

Myth

Dinosaurs and humans lived together.

Fact

About 66 million years separate the last non-bird dinosaurs from the first humans.

Myth

T. rex couldn't see you if you stood still.

Fact

Forward-facing eyes and depth perception possibly better than a hawk's. Standing still would not save you.

Myth

Dinosaurs were slow, scaly and dumb.

Fact

Many were active, warm-blooded and feathered. Troodon had one of the largest brains for its body size.

Myth

Dinosaurs are extinct.

Fact

Birds are dinosaurs, and there are more living dinosaur species today than species of mammals.
